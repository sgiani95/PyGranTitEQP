\noindent Dear Prof. Dr. Pérez-Ramírez,

We wish to submit a new \textsc{Communication} manuscript entitled ``A Green, Reagent-Saving Analytical Strategy for Equivalence Point Determination in Potentiometric Titrations via Gran and Schwartz Linearization''  for consideration by \textsc{Green Chemistry}.

We report a highly original, two-stage analytical strategy that enables precise potentiometric equivalence point determination while systematically reducing titrant consumption by 10–30\,\% per analysis. By automatically identifying an optimal earliest acceptable volume based on user-defined agreement with the full-dataset equivalence point, using Gran–Schwartz linearization, the method achieves excellent repeatability without compromising accuracy. This protocol offers immediate practical utility for routine laboratory, educational, and high-throughput applications.

We believe this contribution fits well within the scope of \textsc{Green Chemistry}, as it develops an innovative analytical methodology that significantly reduces chemical consumption and laboratory waste while advancing the principles of green and sustainable chemistry in everyday laboratory practice. The general significance of the proposed approach, along with its conceptual novelty, aims to engage the journal’s diverse readership.

The manuscript adheres to the journal guidelines for a \textsc{Communication}-type and contains approximately 2000 words, two figures and two tables (with Supplementary Information available). The work is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by any other journal. All authors have approved the manuscript and this submission.

We anticipate that this preliminary \textsc{Communication} will be followed by a full paper presenting broader applications, additional chemical systems, and further validation for different titration types. We believe the present work merits publication as a \textsc{Communication} because it introduces an innovative, readily adoptable concept with important implications for green analytical chemistry (Principles 1, and 5) and has strong potential for rapid uptake by the green chemistry community.

Please address all correspondence regarding this manuscript to me at \texttt{samuele.giani@usz.ch}.

Thank you for considering our manuscript for review. We appreciate your time and look forward to your response.
